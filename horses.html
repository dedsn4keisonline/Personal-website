Crueilty is so often mundane. Atrocities are filed in paperwork and undertaken with chorse. The worst of us have tricked the rest that only grand acts of passion and hatred can be so grim. But evil often has a supply chain designed to make each link feel innocent.

Sometimes the most cruel thing you can do is follow the rules.

Sometimes the most cruel thing you can do is nothing at all.
